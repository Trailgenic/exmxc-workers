Inspect the admission rule, evidence and falsifiers

Every outcome comes from the published sPEG Index evidence object. The interface does not recalculate SDS, change membership or estimate investment returns.

Selected research packets are accessible in the company links above and in the canonical JSON release. The detailed selector activates only if that exact release can be verified.

What the number means

SDS is the integer sum of Constraint, Substitution resistance, Economic capture, Persistence, and Cost of defense only when all five are known. It is ordinal: 16 is not twice 8, and 15 is not 75% confidence. Admission requires C/S/E/P of at least 3, D of at least 2, adequate company materiality, at least Moderate decisive confidence, a complete evidence packet, a current review, and no unresolved material eligibility event.
