AI Commerce Signal Methodology

Version 1.0.0 · September 25, 2026

Measure what an AI assistant did in a shopping journey, what the shopper did next, and what evidence supports each claim.

Return to AI Commerce · Machine-readable method

Unit of analysis

An AI-mediated shopping episode must join an identifiable AI tool, a concrete shopping task, and a product or service category in one attributable, permitted source. Generic product reviews, broad wallet commentary, retailer announcements, and abstract opinions about AI do not qualify.

Journey and autonomy

StageEvidence needed
Discovery and comparisonA person used AI to find or evaluate products.
Recommendation and selectionA suggestion is recorded separately from an explicit choice.
Referral and cartA merchant visit or prepared cart does not prove a transaction.
CheckoutA completed purchase requires a direct claim or instrumented event; agent-executed checkout also requires explicit evidence of delegated authority.
Post-purchaseReturns, support, and satisfaction are attributed to the AI experience only when stated.

Evidence lanes

  • Shopper episodes: verified first-person reports or consented events; self-selection prevents prevalence claims.
  • Surveys: published estimates with their sample, question, geography, field date, and denominator.
  • Referrals: observed AI-sourced visits or conversions, never relabeled as agent-executed purchases.
  • Agent selection: repeatable shopping tasks and captured model responses, separate from consumer behavior.
  • Search interest: a dated Google Trends comparison of “ChatGPT shopping” and “AI shopping assistant,” US Web Search, All categories, past 12 months. The archived CSV and weekly 0–100 series exclude the current incomplete week. These jointly normalized, sampled values are not search volume, AI use, sentiment, or purchases; a new export may have a different scale.

Platform feature announcements describe what is possible; they are not demand observations.

Experience readings

We classify explicit feedback about usefulness, accuracy, trust, price confidence, control, privacy, disclosure, or friction. Product satisfaction is not sentiment toward AI shopping. A public experience reading requires 30 unique episodes, three independent source origins, five collection days, and no origin contributing more than 60%. Otherwise its status is insufficient evidence.

Survey percentages, referral growth, search interest, and episode sentiment are never blended into a composite score.

Foundation and provenance

The September 25 foundation has four attributed Adobe Digital Insights benchmarks. A separate dated pilot adds the Google Trends search-interest export. Both releases have zero qualifying exmxc shopping episodes, controlled selection runs, or instrumented transaction events. Each benchmark retains its source, period, geography, unit, denominator, and interpretation. No agent purchase total is claimed.
